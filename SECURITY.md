# Security Policy

Kodibox handles tenant personal data and payment records, so we take security reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@kodibox.co.ke** (placeholder; address and PGP key to be confirmed before launch).
Please do not open public issues for security problems.

Include: a description, steps to reproduce, affected component, and impact. We aim to acknowledge within 2 business days and triage within 5.

## Ground rules

- Test only against your own accounts or the sandbox organization; never access other organizations' real data.
- No denial-of-service, social engineering of staff or tenants, or data exfiltration beyond minimal proof.
- Give us reasonable time to fix before public disclosure.

Good-faith research that follows these rules will not be pursued by Kodibox (final safe-harbour wording pending legal review).
